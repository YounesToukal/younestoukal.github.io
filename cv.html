Research projects

  1. 2026
    Context-aware hepatic vessel segmentation
    Medical Segmentation Decathlon, Task 08
    A 2.5D EfficientNet U-Net for hepatic vessels in CT, using adjacent slices as context, deep supervision and a clDice loss to keep thin vessels connected.
  2. 2026
    VesselVis — 3D vessel segmentation review tool
    Thesis project, USTHB
    A web tool for reviewing and correcting 3D liver vessel segmentations, with synchronised 2D slice views, a 3D mesh viewer, and relabelling and island removal written back to the volume.
  3. 2026
    Generative models from the ground up
    ACVSS, Accra
    A DDIM sampler with classifier-free guidance built from scratch, followed by ControlNet, LoRA fine-tuning and flow matching on Stable Diffusion 1.5.
  4. 2026
    Depth and 3D reconstruction in an operating room
    Independent project
    Scene geometry and meshes from a single fisheye camera in a medical simulation trauma room, where wide-angle distortion and occlusion break standard pinhole depth pipelines.
  5. 2025
    Video understanding and action recognition
    KTH dataset
    A comparison of action recognition approaches on KTH: frozen DINOv2 features with a temporal head, LSTMs, I3D and video transformers, weighed against their cost.
  6. 2024
    Thermal tempering and thermal shock in glass
    SEDIVER · IPR–ISCR, Rennes
    Residual stress build-up during the thermal tempering of glass insulators, and the conditions under which thermal shock leads to failure.
  7. Ongoing
    Saliency-inspired vision architecture
    Independent research
    A vision architecture whose structure follows visual saliency and attention, instead of attending uniformly over a fixed grid of tokens.
