Deep Learning Indaba

Aug 2026 Lagos, Nigeria Pan-Atlantic University

Deep Learning Indaba is the annual pan-African gathering for the machine learning community — researchers, engineers, students and policymakers from across the continent, working to strengthen Africa's AI research capacity and leadership. The 2026 edition's theme was "Sovereign Intelligence": Africa's capacity to develop and direct its own AI systems, data and scientific future.

I took part in the Indaba's programme of talks, tutorials and community-building around machine learning research in Africa.
