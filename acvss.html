African Computer Vision Summer School

Jul 2026 Accra, Ghana Google AI Community Center

ACVSS is an intensive, hands-on summer school in computer vision for students and early-career researchers from across Africa, combining lectures from researchers in the field with project-based work over the course of the programme.

I worked on the generative-models project described under Selected Work — hand-building a DDIM sampler with classifier-free guidance, then working through ControlNet, LoRA fine-tuning and flow matching on Stable Diffusion 1.5.
